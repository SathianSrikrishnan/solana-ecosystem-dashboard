# Benchmark and North Star

## Direct answer

This problem has been solved in pieces. It has not been solved as one complete,
beginner-readable, source-visible operating view.

## Strong reference products

### Solana Data

Official dashboard: <https://solana.com/data>  
Open aggregator: <https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-data-aggregator>

What it does well:

- standardizes data from several professional providers;
- exposes network, stablecoin, and DeFi metrics;
- publishes the aggregator code and metric methodologies;
- compares providers rather than hiding disagreement.

Current gap relevant to this project:

- refreshes twice daily and intentionally lags by one day;
- focuses on standardized measurements rather than live network operation;
- does not make wallet-versus-human identity ambiguity the central lesson;
- does not combine anomalies, community news, upgrades, and a plain-English
  action briefing in the requested three output formats.

### growthepie

<https://www.growthepie.com/>

What it does well:

- makes Ethereum and Layer 2 activity comparable;
- combines historical and real-time metrics;
- adds short data-driven articles to charts;
- treats analytics as a public ecosystem education product.

### L2BEAT

<https://l2beat.com/scaling/summary>

What it does well:

- makes methodology, risks, stages, and trust assumptions visible;
- values accuracy and uncomfortable caveats over promotional simplicity;
- supports both quick comparison and deep inspection.

### Artemis

<https://www.artemis.xyz/>

What it does well:

- standardizes metrics across chains and applications;
- helps analysts identify apps and sectors with changing usage;
- turns ecosystem data into daily investment and research workflows.

### Token Terminal

<https://tokenterminal.com/terminal/projects/solana>

What it does well:

- describes blockchains using understandable business metrics;
- makes revenue, fees, incentives, expenses, and sustainability comparable;
- supplies explicit metric definitions and methods.

## Product North Star

Combine:

- **Solana Data's** provider transparency;
- **growthepie's** approachable ecosystem storytelling;
- **L2BEAT's** methodological honesty;
- **Artemis's** decision-oriented activity monitoring;
- **Token Terminal's** business-language explanations;
- our own live RPC health, identity ladder, anomaly evidence, AI-assisted
  explanation, and beginner learning mode.

## Winning sentence

This should be the place a Solana builder opens each morning to understand what
changed, what the change might mean, how certain we are, and what deserves
investigation next.

## Anti-goal

Do not build another wall of charts or imply originality by hiding existing
work. Attribute sources, reuse only where licenses allow, and make the original
contribution the interpretation, verification, identity comparison, and
action-oriented workflow.
