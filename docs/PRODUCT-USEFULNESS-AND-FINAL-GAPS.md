# Product usefulness and final gaps

## The simple product

**Six questions. One living system. Every claim inspectable.**

The Observatory is useful when it helps a reader do three things quickly:

1. see what changed across Solana;
2. understand what the measurement can and cannot prove;
3. inspect the source before repeating the claim.

It is not a single health score, an investment recommendation, or a complete
index of every Solana application.

## Why it earns trust

- **Trust:** each metric has a definition, source, collection time, confidence,
  and limitation; failed sources stay visible.
- **Breadth:** six linked layers cover the network, adoption, economy,
  validators, ecosystem, and financial rails without treating one metric as the
  truth.
- **Reproducibility:** one validated JSON snapshot produces the interactive
  HTML and readable Markdown; the collectors, tests, and workflow are public.

## What still limits repeat use

| Gap | Why it matters | Honest next move |
|---|---|---|
| Stale Dune adoption slice | Returning addresses and app overlap are habit-forming signals | Run one bounded free-credit refresh before submission, then choose a sustainable cadence |
| No licensed live RWA value | The financial-rails thesis needs tokenized-asset evidence | Keep the gap visible until licensed API and redistribution rights exist |
| Transfers are not verified payments | Raw stablecoin volume can exaggerate commercial use | Add only an attributable payment taxonomy or provider-supplied dataset |
| No alert or archive habit | A dashboard without a reason to return struggles to retain traffic | After the bounty, add weekly change notes, archives, and opt-in alerts |
| No personal watchlist | Different readers care about different layers | After the bounty, add saved sections or embeddable mini-panels |

## Final bounty scope

Before submission, improve readability, shareability, proof, and the freshness
decision. Do not add speculative metrics or unsafe scraping. After submission,
the highest-leverage product work is a weekly change brief, historical archive,
embeddable cards, and—only with permission—a licensed RWA adapter.

## Why families belong in the story

Financial rails matter when they become dependable enough to support long-term
promises. Tooth Fairy Network is the separate application experiment: family
time capsules and durable incentives on mainnet. The Observatory is the evidence
layer. It tests whether Solana's reliability, cost, adoption, and financial
infrastructure justify that conviction instead of treating conviction as proof.
