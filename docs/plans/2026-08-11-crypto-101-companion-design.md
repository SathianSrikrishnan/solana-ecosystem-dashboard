# Crypto 101 companion page design

## Purpose

Create a short, visual entry point for non-crypto readers who ask why Sathian is
learning and building on Solana. The page is a personal essay first and a
technical doorway second. It links to the Observatory for evidence without
forcing a beginner through all 45 metrics.

## Editorial structure

1. Hero: **Two Kinds of Power**, with an original symbolic banner.
2. Personal hook: twin daughters prompted a way of thinking about paired forces.
3. Metaphor: Saraswati represents knowledge and creative intelligence; Lakshmi
   represents prosperity and abundance. This is presented as Sathian's personal
   shorthand, not a universal statement of Hindu doctrine.
4. Technical bridge: Bitcoin made a public ledger difficult to rewrite;
   Ethereum made agreements programmable; Solana made shared execution cheaper
   and faster; agentic software raises the question of how machines will settle.
5. Honest boundary: low cost and speed involve real validator-hardware,
   operational, and stake-distribution trade-offs.
6. Close: building is how Sathian tests the thesis, with the Observatory and
   Tooth Fairy Network as two practical experiments.

Target body length: 250-350 words. Reading time: about 90 seconds.

## Visual direction

Mood: sacred editorial, restrained and contemporary.

- Ink-black background, warm ivory type, saffron/copper accents, and one cool
  cyan ledger signal.
- A brass balance is the central symbol. Knowledge is represented through a
  manuscript, music, and white light; prosperity through lotus, grain, and
  warm gold. A precise ledger grid sits underneath both.
- Sacred figures are not turned into crypto mascots. No logos in deity hands,
  no Satoshi-as-Brahma image, and no pseudo-script or generated religious text.
- The generated image contains no typography; page text remains accessible HTML.
- Desktop and mobile retain a single editorial reading column with a compact,
  scrollable four-step timeline.

## Draft boundary
