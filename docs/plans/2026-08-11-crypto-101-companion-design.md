# Saraswati, Lakshmi, and the Ledger flagship design

## Purpose

Create a flagship editorial page for readers who know Sathian personally but do
not yet understand why he is learning about AI, crypto, and Solana. The page is
not a crypto sales pitch. It introduces a personal framework, lets readers use
it, and then points to working experiments and primary evidence.

## Editorial promise

**Working title:** *Saraswati, Lakshmi, and the Ledger*

**Thesis:** AI is making capability easier to access, while crypto is making
value easier to program. Neither technology is automatically wise or
prosperous. Sathian uses two inherited questions to judge what deserves to be
built: does it deepen knowledge and discernment, and does it create durable
value and flourishing?

The page uses **two kinds of power** as the memorable family mnemonic. It then
credits Balaji Srinivasan's argument that "AI is polytheistic, not
monotheistic" as a useful modern extension: different models offer different
strengths, limits, incentives, and values. The point is not to catalogue prior
uses of the deity pairing or defend an originality claim.

## Reading architecture

1. **Cinematic hero:** title, compact thesis, and original deity-inspired art.
2. **Personal opening:** Sathian's seven-year-old daughters asking questions
   made inherited symbols newly relevant.
3. **Quick Hinduism orientation:** one careful screen explaining that Hindu
   traditions are plural and the figures cannot be reduced to job titles.
4. **The two tests:** Saraswati asks about truth, learning, and capability;
   Lakshmi asks about value, distribution, and flourishing.
5. **Interactive test:** six reader-controlled questions update a balance and a
   plain-English interpretation. No universal score is claimed.
6. **Swipeable visual reel:** five story frames designed to work in-page now
   and as the source for a later Instagram/LinkedIn carousel.
7. **Crypto 101 rail:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and agentic payments in four
   short steps, with links to deeper writing rather than a technical detour.
8. **Experiments, not endorsements:** Observatory, Tooth Fairy Network, Agent
   Allowance Lab, and the site assistant each test one part of the thesis.
9. **Honest boundary and invitation:** AI can produce confident falsehoods;
   crypto can amplify extraction; ledgers record claims but do not prove the
   outside world. Readers are invited to inspect and challenge the work.

Target main-copy length: 700-950 words. Progressive disclosure keeps the first
reading path under five minutes.

## Visual direction

**Style:** editorial grid / magazine, mixed with restrained anti-polish texture.

**Memorable device:** a living balance line runs through the page. It becomes
the interactive test rather than serving as decoration.

**Palette:** carbon `#0A0907`, paper `#F1E8D5`, vermilion `#B43B2F`, temple
brass `#C89A43`, river cyan `#65D4D0`, and muted stone `#A69B87`.

**Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for large editorial headings and Libre
Baskerville for reading copy, with a compact sans-serif utility face for labels.
Local fallbacks keep the page usable when web fonts are unavailable.

**Image rhythm:**

- one wide generated hero joining manuscript, lotus, circuitry, and ledger;
- one Saraswati-focused portrait and one Lakshmi-focused portrait;
- one modern agentic-ledger image;
- the existing brass-balance artwork as a connective chapter image;
- one personal photograph before publication on sathian.ai.

Literal deity imagery is presented as contemporary generated interpretation,
not canonical iconography. Captions make that boundary explicit. Logos appear
only in the technical rail, never in deity imagery.

## Interaction boundary

- No account, analytics, storage, wallet connection, or financial advice.
- The self-test operates entirely in the browser and resets on reload.
- Every control is keyboard accessible and the result is announced to assistive
  technology.
- The visual reel uses native scrolling first; previous/next controls enhance
  it without making JavaScript mandatory.

## Release boundary

This remains a local review draft. It will not be deployed, added to sathian.ai,
or posted socially until Sathian approves the copy, images, cultural framing,
and final personal photograph. The second review must specifically confirm the
personal opening and the phrase "a pantheon of imperfect intelligences."
