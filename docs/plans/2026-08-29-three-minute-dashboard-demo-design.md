# Three-minute Solana Observatory demo design

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: awaiting narration-script approval

## Outcome

Produce a private three-minute demonstration of Solana Observatory that can be
understood without prior Solana knowledge. The screen recording is captured
first as a clean browser-only performance. Sathian records the approved
narration and camera take separately. The edit then combines the deterministic
browser movement, narration, and a restrained picture-in-picture treatment.

## Capture architecture

The browser performance follows nine timed cues: Overview, Network, Adoption,
Economy, Validators, Ecosystem, Financial rails, Methods, and a return to the
Overview for the close. Browser control uses existing navigation anchors and
ordinary cursor movement; it does not modify the public page or add temporary
annotations. Clipchamp records only the uniquely identified Solana dashboard
Chrome window at 1080p. Other open or minimized applications are outside the
capture boundary.

The narration is approximately 388 words, calibrated to three minutes at about
129 words per minute. Each browser cue begins when its matching paragraph
begins. The clean screen recording can be tightened in post if the spoken take
runs slightly fast or slow.

## Editorial contract

The demo shows representative evidence rather than attempting to enumerate all
45 metrics. It describes the data state honestly: 36 metrics are current, five
Dune adoption metrics preserve their last verified values as stale, and four
research gaps remain unavailable. Stale and unavailable are not described as
broken. The script distinguishes wallets from people, measurements from health
verdicts, and raw transfers from identifiable payments.

## Safety and approval gates

- Save raw and edited recordings locally under `C:\Users\sathi\Videos`.
- Do not capture the desktop, private tabs, messages, calendar, wallets, or
  unrelated windows.
- Do not upload, publish, share, or submit the recording.
- Gate 1: owner approves the narration and timing.
- Gate 2: record and inspect the browser-only performance.
- Gate 3: owner records narration/camera and approves the private composite.
- Gate 4: add the final approved URL to the bounty package; submission remains
  a separate action-time approval.
